About Jeon Wonwoo

Jeon Wonwoo was born on July 17, 1996, in Changwon, South Korea. He is the lead rapper and sub-vocalist of the South Korean boy group SEVENTEEN, under PLEDIS Entertainment.

Known for his deep voice, sharp visual aesthetics, and love for gaming and reading, Wonwoo has captured the hearts of fans worldwide.
